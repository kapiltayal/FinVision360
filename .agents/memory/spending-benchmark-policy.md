---
name: Spending benchmark interpretation
description: Scope and interpretation of surveyed spending comparisons.
---

Treat spending benchmarks as descriptive survey averages, not financial targets or personalized peer rankings. Display the survey year, source, comparison period and unmatched coverage.

**Why:** The user requested a comparison against surveyed spending patterns. The initial national survey choice is not evidence about the user's household demographics or an endorsement of higher/lower spending.

**How to apply:** Only compare categories with defensible scope alignment. Do not silently map broad buckets such as Shopping or Insurance to narrower survey categories, treat missing charges as proven zero, or imply the benchmark is adjusted for inflation, income, location or household size when it is not.
